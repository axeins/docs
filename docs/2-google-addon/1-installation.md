# Installation

### **Option 1: Install from Google Workspace Marketplace**

1. Go to the [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace).
2. Search for **"AX1"** and click **Install**.

### **Option 2: Install Directly from Google Sheets**

1. Open your Google Sheet.
2. Click **Extensions > Add-ons > Get add-ons**.

   ![Google marketplace](/img/google-marketplace.png)

3. Search for **"AX1"** and click **Install**.
